Sheet 04 · Propulsion

Raptor 3

Every Starship flight rides on these engines. Raptor burns liquid methane and liquid oxygen in a full-flow staged combustion cycle: essentially all of both propellants passes through a turbine before it reaches the main chamber, and nothing is thrown overboard. It is the most efficient way to run a rocket's pumps, and the hardest to build. Raptor was the first engine of its kind to fly.
